Korean OMR handwriting recognition model

Model: korean_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec (PaddleOCR)
Source: https://huggingface.co/monkt/paddleocr-onnx/tree/main/languages/korean
Original project: https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
License: Apache License 2.0
https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/main/LICENSE

The ONNX model and character dictionary are loaded in the user's browser.
OMR photos are processed locally and are not published in this repository.
